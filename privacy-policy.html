Privacy Policy for Substack Downloader

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Substack Downloader is an application designed to help users download media content from publicly accessible Substack pages and posts. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

Information We Collect

Substack Downloader does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, addresses, passwords, or payment information.

How the Application Works

The application may connect to the internet to retrieve publicly accessible content requested by the user. URLs entered by the user are used only to process the requested download.

Downloaded Files

Files downloaded through the application are stored locally on the user's device. The application does not intentionally upload downloaded files to our servers.

Internet Connection

The application requires an internet connection for downloading content. Network requests may be sent to third-party websites or services required to retrieve the content requested by the user.

Third-Party Services

The application may interact with third-party websites or services. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms of use. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party service accessed through the application.

Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the application developer through the contact information provided on the application's official page.
